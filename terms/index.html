Terms

Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version: Resonance is free to use. Resonance+ is an optional subscription that covers both of you. You can cancel whenever you like, and if you change your mind in the first 14 days, we’ll refund you in full.

Who we are

Resonance is run by Global Tether Ventures Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16765434). Our registered office is 45 Silverstream House, Fitzroy Street, London W1T 6EB. You can reach us any time at contact@sharedresonance.uk.

Using Resonance

Resonance is a private space for two people. You need to be 16 or over to use it. Please keep your login to yourself, and don’t use Resonance to harass anyone or share anything illegal. If you do, we may close your account. How we handle your information is in our privacy policy.

Resonance+

Resonance+ adds extra features on top of the free app. One of you pays and you both get it. Whatever you choose, prices include any tax.

  • Monthly (£7.99) and yearly (£74.99) plans renew automatically until you cancel. We’ll email you a receipt each time.
  • Lifetime (£179.99) is one payment, and Resonance+ stays on for as long as Resonance runs.

The subscription belongs to the person who pays for it. If you unpair, it stays with them and covers whoever they pair with next.

Cancelling

You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time from the Resonance+ page, under Manage subscription. You keep Resonance+ until the end of the time you’ve already paid for, and it won’t renew after that. Nothing you’ve made together is deleted.

Refunds

If you change your mind within 14 days of your first payment, on any plan, email us and we’ll refund you in full. After that, payments aren’t refunded part-way through a month or year, but you can always cancel so you aren’t charged again. This doesn’t affect your legal rights, for example if something doesn’t work as described.

Printed postcards

Monthly and yearly plans include one printed postcard a month, which doesn’t carry over to the next month. Lifetime includes 12 printed postcards that never expire. We print them and send them by post to the address your partner gives us. How long the post takes is out of our hands, but if a card doesn’t arrive, tell us and we’ll send it again.

If prices change

If we change the price of a plan you’re on, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before it applies, so you can cancel first if you’d rather. Lifetime is never charged again.

If Resonance ever closes

We’d give you at least 60 days’ notice and a way to download what you’ve made together. We’d refund any monthly or yearly time you’d paid for and not used. If you bought lifetime in the two years before we closed, we’d refund part of it, in proportion to how much of those two years was left.

The legal bits

If we change these terms, we’ll update the date at the top and, for anything important, let you know in the app first.

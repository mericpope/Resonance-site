THE LONGEST VALENTINE’S

Your Valentine’s lasts 29 hours.

It starts at midnight in London and ends at midnight in New York, five hours later. Resonance shows the whole stretch on Home, from the moment it begins for one of you to the moment it ends for the other.

In the app, the week before

From 7 February, Home reminds you to write your promise, and you hide your notes from the same screen. On the evening of the 13th it counts down to midnight in whichever of your cities gets there first. Nothing to set up.

More ideas for the day

  • Add a word to your world that only the two of you understand.
  • Answer the same question that morning and compare at night.
  • Pick a photo theme: “something that reminds me of you”.
  • Add your next trip and watch the little plane head their way.
  • Write down your firsts: the first message, the first visit, the first “I love you”.
  • Start a Someday list of everywhere you want to go, together.

Questions

Do we both need Resonance?+

Yes, one account each. It’s free, on iPhone, Android and the web at sharedresonance.app, and one of you invites the other.

Is any of this only for Valentine’s Day?+

No. Postcards, letters, the window, holding hands and the lamp are there every day. Valentine’s is just a good excuse to use them all at once.

However far apart you are, make the 14th feel close.

Small moments, every day

Not another chat app. A quiet place that is just yours, built around the little things you would share if you lived down the hall.

1

Pair up

One of you creates the space and shares a short invite code. The other joins. It only ever holds two people.

2

Share the day

Set your mood word, answer the question, take today's photos. Each of you sees the other's world in their own time.

3

Build something together

Photos become a mosaic. Solved words become your own dictionary. A countdown carries you to the next trip.

Everything you share, nothing you don't

Both clocks

Their time and yours, side by side. You always know if it is a good moment to call.

Daily pulse check

One word, once a day. "Partner is golden today." No explaining needed, and no pressure to reply.

Daily photos

A shared theme and a handful of colours to hunt for. Photos stay hidden until you have both posted.

Your own dictionary

Send a word and your partner guesses it letter by letter. Every solved word goes in the book, with photos to further build out your world.

Trips and countdown

Plan the next visit together, with a progress bar that fills as the day gets closer.

One question a day

Learn new things about each other by answering questions and comparing responses.

Thinking of you

One tap sends a heart. It lands on their phone, and a heart blooms on their home screen when they open the app.

Dates that matter

Anniversaries, birthdays, the day you met. They show up on your home screen the week before, with a nudge on the day.

The month-end reveal

At the end of the month, small moments converge into a bigger picture that you created together.

The monthly mosaic

A month of ordinary days, one picture

Each day one of you picks a theme, and you each get five shades to find for it. Every photo you take becomes one tile in a mosaic the two of you are building.

One of you secretly chooses the picture and day by day it comes into focus. At the end of the month the whole thing is revealed.

Step back and you see the picture. Zoom in and every tile is still one of your photos.

Private, By Design

It is a space for two people. We kept it that way on purpose.

No location tracking

Resonance never asks where you are. It only knows your time zone, so the clock is right.

Revealed together

Photos and answers stay hidden until you have both posted, so neither of you is reacting to the other.

Just the two of you

No feed, no followers, no public profile. Everything lives inside your world.

No ads, ever

We don't sell your data or track you across other apps. Delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Questions

Is Resonance only for couples?+

Yes. Resonance is built for couples who live apart. Each space holds exactly two people: you and your partner.

Does it track my location?+

No. It uses your time zone to show the right time, and nothing more about where you are.

When can I get it?+

Soon, on iPhone and Android. Join the early access list and we will email you as soon as it's ready.

Do we both need iPhones?+

No. One of you can be on iPhone and the other on Android. Everything syncs between you.

Can I delete my account?+

Yes, at any time. Open the menu in the app and choose Delete my account. Your account, photos and answers are removed straight away.

How do I get help?+

Email contact@sharedresonance.uk and we'll get back to you. There's more on the support page.

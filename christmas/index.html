How a door opens

  1. Fill your half the evening before. From 5pm, tomorrow’s door is ready. Answer the question or take the photo. It takes a minute.
  2. Your partner wakes up to it. When their day starts, your half is waiting for them, and they add theirs.
  3. The door opens. Both halves sit side by side: your answer and theirs, your photo and theirs.

On Christmas morning, a card arrives

Behind door 25 is an envelope. Open it and your card slides out: the photos of you both, your December in numbers, your two songs, the same moment from two cities, and how many days until you’re together again. It stays in the app to look back on, and with Resonance+ every year’s card is kept, so next Christmas can tell you how far you’ve come.

Wherever midnight is

Each door opens when the day starts where you are. If your partner is five hours ahead, their morning comes first, and your half is already there for them.

Call it what you like

Advent, a countdown to Christmas or just December. The doors are about the two of you, and nothing in them asks you to celebrate anything in particular.

30November, the evening it starts

Already on Resonance? It appears on your phone on the evening of 30 November, ready for door 1. New here? Join before then and start on day one.

Questions

Is it free?+

Yes. Every door, and the card, is free for every couple on Resonance. With Resonance+ you can also record your Christmas Eve letter in your own voice, and every December’s card is kept (free couples keep their latest one).

What if we miss a day?+

Doors stay open until 6 January, so you can catch up whenever you like.

Do we both need Resonance?+

Yes. It works on iPhone, Android and the web at sharedresonance.app, and one of you invites the other.

Can my partner see my half before I’ve seen theirs?+

They see it once their day for that door has started, so whoever fills in first leaves something waiting. Christmas Eve letters stay sealed for you both until door 24.

However far apart you are this December, open the doors together.

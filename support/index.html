Getting started

How do my partner and I pair up?+

One of you signs up and opens Invite your partner to get a short code. The other signs up, chooses I have a code, and enters it. A space only ever holds two people.

I didn't get the confirmation email+

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from Resonance. If it isn't there after a few minutes, try signing up again with the same address to get a new link.

I forgot my password+

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. The link in the email lets you choose a new password.

Using Resonance

How does the daily photo hunt work?+

Each day one of you picks a theme, taking turns. You each get the theme plus a few colours to find. Your photos stay hidden from each other until you've both posted, and each one becomes a tile in your monthly mosaic.

How does the word game work?+

Send your partner a word, with a photo clue if you like. They guess it letter by letter: each guess shows which letters are in the right place (green), somewhere else in the word (a gold ring) or not in it at all (dark red hatching). Letters in the right place stay filled in, and a photo clue gets clearer with every guess. Solved words go into Your world.

I'm not getting notifications+

In Resonance, open the menu and check Notifications. If it says they're blocked, tap it to open your phone's settings and allow notifications for Resonance.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?+

Open the menu in the app and choose Delete my account. Your account, photos, answers and moods are removed straight away. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Who can see what I share?+

Only your partner. There's no feed, no public profile and no ads. The privacy policy has the details.

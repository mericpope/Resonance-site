What it is

A private app for the two of you, on iPhone, Android and the web. No feed, no followers, no ads.

Both time zonesTheir time, sunset and weather, always beside yours.
Something new each dayA question to answer together, and photos that build to a mosaic.
Little check-insA heart, a hug or a goodnight in one tap, and a word for how your day is going.
Counting down togetherThe days until you next see each other, and every birthday and anniversary in between.

Also in Resonance: a shared space with a lamp, sofa, window, and hands to hold; digital postcards and letters; your own world of words; your firsts and future lists; and a recap of your week together every Sunday.

What I’m asking

  • Use it together for a month, the way you normally would.
  • Tell me what confused you, what you loved, and what you wish it did. A couple of sentences is plenty.
  • That’s it. You don’t have to post about it, and you can leave whenever you like.
50founding places
  • Six months of Resonance+, free, for both of you.
  • Then a founders’ price, for as long as you stay: £5.99 a month or £59.90 a year (2 months free).
  • A direct line to me: a WhatsApp group for founding couples, where your ideas shape what I build next. Joining is optional.

I’ll email you within a day or two with your invite: the iPhone or Android test version, your founding code, and the WhatsApp group link. You can also start straight away on the web at sharedresonance.app.

Your privacy

Everything you share is only for the two of you. I never sell your data, show ads or track where you are; Resonance only knows the city you choose. Calls on the sofa and the window go directly between you and are never recorded. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from the app at any time. The details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Do we both need to sign up?+

Yes, one account each. One of you saves a place here; once you’re in, you send your partner an invite code from the app. The free Resonance+ covers you both.

What happens after six months?+

You keep Resonance+ at the founders’ price if you’d like to, or go back to the free app. Nothing you’ve made together is ever deleted.

Do we have to be long distance?+

Not at all. Opposite shifts, work trips, a term at university: if you spend time apart, it’s for you.

Is it on the App Store?+

Not yet. Founding couples get the iPhone and Android test versions by email, and it works on the web at sharedresonance.app in the meantime.

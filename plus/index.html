Questions

Do we both need to pay?+

Nope. One of you signs up and you’re both in.

What happens to the free app?+

Nothing changes. Everything you use today stays free, Resonance+ just adds to it.

Does “their sky” track where we are?+

No. It goes off the city you’ve each put in your profile, never your location. The weather service only ever sees the name of the city.

Can my partner read a letter before opening it?+

No. Letters stay sealed until they’re opened, and only the two of you can ever read them.

Does it work if one of us has an iPhone and the other an Android?+

Yes. Resonance works on iPhone, Android and the web, and everything you send reaches each other however you’re both connected.

Do we have to be long distance?+

Not at all. It’s for any time you’re apart, whether that’s different countries, work trips or just opposite shifts.

Will you add more?+

Yes, we’re always building. If there’s something you wish Resonance could do, tell us at contact@sharedresonance.uk.

How much will it cost?+

We’re still working that out. If you’re on the early access list, you’ll hear first.

Want in early?

Resonance+ is on its way to the App Store and Google Play. Pop your email on the early access list and we’ll let you know as soon as it’s ready.
